# The spatial and combination headline intervals, conditioned on the model's own viability constraint

Generated by `scripts/headline_intervals_under_admissibility.py`. `scripts/headline_uncertainty.py` publishes prior-predictive 95% intervals for several headline families -- the RSL3 + FSP1i Bliss synergy, the two spatial `sim-tme` headlines, and the per-tissue vessel-wall kill -- by sweeping a joint uniform prior over the same PRCC rate constants the single-cell design uses. `intervals-under-admissibility.md` (#936) conditioned the single-cell intervals on the manuscript's own baseline-viability constraint and left these explicitly outstanding. This applies the same conditioning to them.

## The constraint, and what it leaves of each family's prior

A draw is INADMISSIBLE when its worst untreated phenotype reaches the manuscript's stated ceiling of 2% death under Control -- the same ceiling, imported from the same generator, that `headline_at_fitted.py` uses to reject the in-vitro-fitted cascade, judged on `Glycolytic/Control`, `Persister/Control`, `PersisterNrf2/Control` at the published per-draw cell count. The untreated arms do not appear anywhere in the published design, so a draw's admissibility was not knowable from any committed artifact.

| family | published draws | admissible | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| bliss | 300 | 125 | 41.7% |
| tme | 100 | 36 | 36.0% |
| penetration | 300 | 125 | 41.7% |

Over the widest draw set the median worst-untreated death rate is 3.3% and 23 draws kill more than half the untreated population before any treatment is applied.

Those rows are NESTED, not independent: `bliss` and `penetration` draw the same rows, and the smaller sample is a PREFIX of the larger, so there are 2 distinct draw sets here rather than 3. That is deliberate -- one admissibility pass then covers every family, and the partitions cannot disagree because of sampling.

The prior is ONE box, so these are samples of one quantity that the sibling page measured on 2000 draws (37.7%). Checked rather than asserted: the largest deviation here is 1.42 standard errors against a bar of 3, so the two pages **agree** about the prior. A real disagreement would mean the two definitions of admissible had drifted apart, which is the failure this check exists for.

**Those tails are withheld, and the reason is structural.** Quoting a conditioned tail needs at least 100 admissible draws (the sibling page's floor, imported rather than chosen here), and the `tme` family's published design draws 100 in TOTAL because each costs minutes. So it could only ever meet that floor if the constraint removed NOTHING from it: in any world where conditioning does anything at all, this family cannot quote a conditioned tail. Here it leaves 36, and a 95% tail resting on that many draws is not a bound. Its median is quoted and its tails are not. The published report already says to read its median rather than its bounds; this says the conditioned version cannot offer bounds at all at that draw count.

### The correction does not run one way

Admissibility keeps the draws whose defences are strong enough that an untreated cell survives, which is a single mechanism -- and across these headlines it moves 5 medians DOWN and 1 UP. Down: Hypoxia kill-collapse gap (SDT minus RSL3), SDT immune kill fraction (final counts), Vessel-wall RSL3 kill: well-vascularized, Vessel-wall RSL3 kill: poorly-vascularized, Vessel-wall RSL3 kill: CNS / BBB. Up: Bliss synergy (RSL3 + FSP1i). Reporting whichever was looked at first would have flattered the page in whichever direction that happened to be.

The sharpest consequence is where this leaves the manuscript's own unperturbed point estimate. For **Bliss synergy (RSL3 + FSP1i)** (point 1.992, full-prior median 1.348, admissible median 2.529), **Vessel-wall RSL3 kill: well-vascularized** (point 0.121, full-prior median 0.231, admissible median 0.053), **Vessel-wall RSL3 kill: poorly-vascularized** (point 0.026, full-prior median 0.076, admissible median 0.009), **Vessel-wall RSL3 kill: CNS / BBB** (point 0.018, full-prior median 0.043, admissible median 0.004) the median crosses from one side of the published point to the other, so the conditioned prior disagrees with the full prior about whether the headline figure is optimistic or pessimistic.

The published point estimate still falls inside the admissible interval for 4 of 6 observables, and cannot be placed either way for Hypoxia kill-collapse gap (SDT minus RSL3), SDT immune kill fraction (final counts), whose tails are withheld above.

## Is the Bliss move real, or is its denominator collapsing?

`synergy_score` is a RATIO of the combination kill to the Bliss independence prediction, and conditioning lowers kill, so a rise in the ratio could be the numerator holding or the denominator falling away. The parts come from the same row as the score, so the question is answerable rather than arguable.

| part | full-prior median | admissible median | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bliss_rate_rsl3` | 0.571 | 0.232 | 0.406 |
| `bliss_rate_fsp1i` | 0.087 | 0.008 | 0.092 |
| `bliss_rate_combo` | 0.919 | 0.680 | 0.740 |
| `bliss_prediction` | 0.667 | 0.250 | 0.374 |

Both move down. The denominator falls to 0.374 of its full-prior median and the numerator to 0.740, so the ratio rises **because the Bliss baseline falls faster than the combination kill does** -- the denominator is the larger mover. That is a mechanism, not a measurement of more synergy, and it is the honest reading of the rise.

The engine refuses the ratio entirely below a Bliss baseline of 0.0010 (parsed from `sim-combo-mech`, not typed here). The smallest admissible denominator is 0.0050, which clears it, so the conditioned interval is not being computed next to the undefined corner. The engine's own identity for that baseline reproduces on the stored parts to 0.000000000, so the columns are the parts they are labelled as.

### And the lower bound rises for a different reason

The published report's conclusion is about the lower BOUND rather than the median: it reads the supra-additive direction as robust because the interval does not reach below the no-synergy line at 1.0, where the combination kill exactly equals the independence prediction. 6 of the full prior's draws sit at or below that line, and **not one of them is admissible**. So the sub-additive corner of this prior is entirely made of draws the model itself rejects, which is why the conditioned lower end clears the line.

And the draws sitting EXACTLY on the line are there for a mechanical reason rather than a biological one: every one of them has a single-agent RSL3 kill of at least 1.000, so both arms saturate, the independence prediction is driven to its own ceiling, and the ratio is 1.0 by arithmetic. A saturated arm is also the easiest way to fail the untreated constraint, which is why these are the first draws conditioning removes.

The two Bliss movements are therefore NOT one finding: the MEDIAN rises because the denominator falls faster than the numerator, and the LOWER BOUND rises because the sub-additive corner is inadmissible. Only the second strengthens the published conclusion, and conflating them would read as this page having found more synergy.

**Two sites draw a conclusion from those sub-additive draws, and they are NOT edited here**: `README.md`, `article/drafts/v1.md` each say the interval reaches the additive null and that some draws fall below it, so synergy is not guaranteed. That is TRUE of the full prior they cite and this page does not contradict it -- but every one of those draws is inadmissible, so the conclusion rests on a corner the model rejects. They are handwritten interpretation rather than generated prose, so they are named here rather than rewritten, and the naming is checked against their current text so it cannot outlive the sentences it points at.

## The ordering check, registered in advance as NOT evidence

The penetration report's load-bearing claim is a within-draw ordering, which the penetration-access analysis (#926) showed to be robust BY CONSTRUCTION: one shared monotone dose-response read at decreasing fixed concentrations, so conditioning CANNOT break it, and that was registered on the issue before any observable here existed. Measured: 100.0% of 300 full-prior draws and 100.0% of 125 admissible ones. The full-prior figure reproduces the published one. Read it as a check that this harness evaluates the same observable, not as the ordering surviving a test.

## Does this move any per-headline verdict?

`identifiability-report.md` reads these intervals for its per-headline verdicts, and those are DIRECTION claims rather than magnitudes -- so what would move one is a SIGN change, not a shifted quantile. Measured: no conditioned median changes sign, so no verdict in that report is contradicted by this page. The magnitudes it already declines to quote are the ones that move.

## What this does NOT establish

**Conditioning is not inference.** Removing draws the model itself declares invalid adds no data. It cannot turn a prior-predictive interval into a data-conditioned one, and `identifiability-report.json` still reports 0 data-conditioned headline outputs -- unchanged by everything on this page, and guarded, so this refusal breaks if that report ever says otherwise.

**The admissible subset is not a posterior.** It is the same uniform box with a region removed, so its median is a property of what remains rather than an estimate of anything.

**The constraint is a modelling choice**, stated in the manuscript's prose and imported from the generator whose own guard parses it from there. It is not an experimental observation.

**The `tme` family cannot be given a conditioned interval at its published draw count.** It draws 100 because each costs minutes, and conditioning leaves 36. Its median is reported and its tails are withheld, which is a statement about the published design's resolution rather than about the biology.

**Nothing here recalibrates a parameter.** The prior's ranges are unchanged; only draws the model rejects are removed.

## Verdict

6 of 6 headline observables are reportable -- each reproduces what is published and has admissible draws to condition on -- of which 4 can also have their tails quoted, and 4 carry at least one material shift among the quantiles this page will quote. Overall: **reportable**.
