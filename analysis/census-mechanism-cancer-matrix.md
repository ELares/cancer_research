# The mechanism-by-site matrix at census scale

Generated by `scripts/census_mechanism_cancer_matrix.py`. The universe is the 99,459 census articles carrying BOTH a mechanism descriptor and an anatomical-site descriptor; both marginals are counted over that same universe, because an expectation computed against a population most of the matrix could never enter is not an expectation.

## Zero cells: the original question, answered and retired

The manuscript counted **94 of 418 cells (22.5%)** empty over a retrieved corpus, and its own sensitivity analysis found the count falls to 29-38 under a coarser taxonomy. At census scale it is **6 of 288 (2.1%)**: `hifu/leukaemia`, `hifu/lymphoma`, `hifu/oesophagus`, `microbiome/thyroid`, `mrna-vaccine/oesophagus`, `mrna-vaccine/thyroid` So the empty cells were overwhelmingly a property of the retrieval, which is what the manuscript suspected and could not show.

That also RETIRES the measure. With 4.4 million articles nearly every mechanism-site pair has been written about at least once, so counting zeros no longer discriminates between a neglected combination and a well-studied one. A gap measure that returns 2% on the full literature is not detecting gaps.

### How site-specific each mechanism is

Spread is the standard deviation of a mechanism's ratios across the sites where its expectation is interpretable, shown for the 14 mechanisms clearing 8 such cells. It summarises a whole row without needing any single cell to be extreme, and it separates a therapy aimed at particular organs from a platform or a process that appears wherever cancer is studied.

**The obvious objection, tested.** A small mechanism spread over eighteen sites has small expectations per cell and noisier ratios, so the tails could be a property of the denominator rather than of the field. Correlation between a mechanism's size and its spread is **-0.33** over 14 mechanisms, so size does not predict spread and the ranking is not a small-number artifact.

**`hifu`, `sonodynamic` appear in BOTH tails, and that is one fact rather than two.** A row's ratios are constrained: concentrating a fixed literature into a few sites forces depletion in the rest. So for these mechanisms the depleted cells are the arithmetic shadow of the concentrated ones, and reading the two tables as independent evidence would double-count a single pattern.

## No p-value, deliberately

The null here is that a mechanism's literature is spread across sites independently of which site it is, and that null is KNOWN FALSE before any data is read -- CAR-T concentrates in leukaemia because that is what it treats. A test against it would return significance for nearly every cell and would be testing a hypothesis nobody holds. The ratio is reported as an effect size and the ranking is what carries.

## A depleted cell is not a gap

This analysis names no opportunities, and the restraint is not modesty. An ablative modality is depleted in disseminated disease because there is nothing to ablate -- the modality working as designed, not an oversight. Separating *nobody has tried this* from *this cannot work here* needs knowledge of the mechanism, and the counts do not carry it. What the table supplies is a ranked list of places where the literature is thinner than arithmetic predicts, which is where such a judgement would start.

## Two limits inherited from the labels

The matrix has 16 mechanisms, not the manuscript's 19, because a mechanism MeSH cannot express cannot appear in it at all. TTFields and bioelectric modulation are ABSENT rather than empty, and their absence is invisible in a zero count -- which is worth stating precisely because this page reports the zero count falling.

Sites come from NLM's C04 tree and mechanisms from descriptor sets of varying breadth, so a cell inherits both. A broad mechanism descriptor spreads across sites more evenly than the therapy it names, which flattens its row toward a ratio of 1 and makes it look less concentrated than it is.
