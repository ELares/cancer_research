## Historical adjudication

The embedded historical summary contains 32 title-only labels. Its reported classifier agreement is **34.4%**, with **7** direction reversals; the stored reasons mark 5 of those as cancer-context. These are historical summary values, not a verified accuracy estimate for a newly selected census.

The historical adjudication labels 11 articles protective and 7 sensitising: a protective share of **61.1%** over 18 directional articles.

The adjudication reverses the recorded majority direction: the protective share changes from 37.5% in the regex counts to 61.1% in the adjudication.

The historical calculation reports a 95% Wilson interval of 38.6-79.7%. It does not account for candidate selection, missed articles, adjudication errors, or unverified record linkage.

That interval is wide enough to contain an even split. These counts do not establish their ratio in the broader literature or validate the manuscript's framing.

## Why the regex only generates candidates

The regex is demoted to generating candidates because **"hypoxia-induced ferroptosis resistance" CONTAINS "hypoxia-induced ferroptosis"**. A phrase asserting protection contains a substring that the classifier reads as sensitisation. The retained historical labels document this failure.

## What this cannot do

Settle the biology. A count of reported framings does not establish how hypoxia affects ferroptosis in a particular cell type, inducer, or pathway perturbation. It does not validate the simulation.
