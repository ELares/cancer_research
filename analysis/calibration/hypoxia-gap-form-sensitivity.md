# The O2-form axis the §7.1 interval holds fixed

Generated by `scripts/hypoxia_gap_form_sensitivity.py`. The §7.1 kill-collapse gap is `SDT - RSL3` hypoxic-zone kill, extracted by the same `extract_tme_observables` the published interval uses.

The published 95% prior-predictive interval is [0.163, 0.999] (median 0.96, default point 0.866), computed by sampling the 11 PRCC rate constants. **It never passes `--sdt-o2-dependence`**, so every draw sits at `0.0`, where SDT keeps full hypoxic efficacy.

## Measured

| `--sdt-o2-dependence` | §7.1 gap | vs published interval |
|---|--:|---|
| 0 | +0.866065 | inside |
| 0.25 | +0.636674 | inside |
| 0.5 | +0.291294 | inside |
| 0.75 | +0.046936 | **outside** |
| 1 | -0.000097 | **outside** |

The zero-dependence row reproduces the published default point (0.866) to within 5e-3, so these runs are comparable with the interval rather than a different measurement.

## What it means

**The gap changes sign across the form axis** (+0.866065 at zero dependence, -0.000097 at full), and the full-dependence value falls outside the published interval. A difference that collapses through zero does not have a robust sign to report, so the hypoxia row's `direction_robust` reading is scoped to the rate constants at a fixed form, not to the form itself.

The magnitude collapse was already recorded in `identifiability-report.md` ("~87.8% to ~0%"). What is added here is the **sign** at the collapsed end, which is what the verdict label turns on.

## Scope

- sim-tme carries only the LINEAR `o2_dependent_exo_factor`; the measured OER hyperbola (#726) is not expressible in this binary, so the axis here is off-to-linear. sim-tme-3d covers the OER form.
- Rate constants stay at their defaults, so this varies the form alone and is the complement of the #332 sweep, not a replacement. No joint sweep is attempted.
- `sdt_ros` remains the uncalibrated #336 knob. What varies is the SHAPE of the oxygen response, not the dose.
