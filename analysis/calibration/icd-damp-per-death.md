# The per-dead-cell DAMP advantage, measured

Generated by `scripts/icd_damp_per_death.py`. The engine documented a per-dead-cell DAMP advantage for physical ROS death over GPX4 inhibition, and two committed documents put a number on it. This page measures it in every regime the repository can run.

## The documented predictions

| source | prediction | reached? |
|---|---|---|
| `simulations/sim-icd/README.md` (stated as the binary's key finding, under "Reproducing manuscript claims") | 1.5-2x | no |
| `plans/simulation-suite-v2-plan.md` (registered under "Expected Result" for Simulation 3) | 2-5x | no |

## Measured

| regime | SDT | RSL3 | SDT:RSL3 |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| 2D spatial, release LP | 19.1230 | 18.2658 | **1.0469** |
| sim-icd, OXPHOS | 21.4580 | 19.9650 | **1.0748** |
| sim-icd, Persister | 20.0846 | 17.7240 | **1.1332** |
| panel sweep, 32 readable rows | n/a | n/a | 1.0674 - 1.1475 |

Across 35 readable comparisons the ratio spans **1.0469x to 1.1475x**. The lowest documented prediction is 1.5x, so the best measurement falls short of the weakest claim by a factor of 1.31.

## The direction, which is not what fails

SDT exceeds RSL3 in **all 35** readable comparisons. The documented direction survives; the documented magnitude does not, and this page withdraws only the second.

## The mechanism names a moment where the contrast does not exist

| arm | mean LP at death | mean LP at release | deaths |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Control | 10.1823 | 17.4509 | 1 |
| RSL3 | 10.2474 | 18.2658 | 163 |
| SDT | 10.2657 | 19.1230 | 139,640 |

`death_threshold` is 10, read from `params.rs` rather than quoted. At the moment of death the two arms differ by a factor of 1.0018; after the post-death grace window they differ by 1.0469.

**Both arms are within 10% of the threshold at death**, so a contrast between one arm 'MUCH higher than threshold' and another 'NEAR threshold' cannot be read at that moment: death IS the threshold crossing. The documented contrast compares one arm at death with the other after it.

## Two defects the measurement surfaced

**An absent cohort is not a potency of zero.** 72 of 256 arm-rows in the panel sweep have no cohort to average over
  -- every one of them at a kill count of exactly zero, confirmed against the `deaths` denominator now emitted beside the value. They are reported as `null`; they used to be reported as `0.0`, which under a column headed DAMP-per-death reads as a measurement.

**The ROS cascade is not top of the per-death ranking.** `AntibodyDrugConjugate` releases more per death than SDT in 8 of 64 sweep rows, all of them hypoxic -- a cytotoxic payload out-releasing the cascade wherever oxygen limits it. So 'quality of death' does not order the arms by death mode the way the documented sentence implies.

## A companion claim that inverts for a structural reason

`sim-icd`'s README also stated that SDT+anti-PD-1 immune kills far exceed RSL3's. Measured: SDT 3 against RSL3 57,591. `immune_cascade` caps its kills at the SURVIVING population, and 4 of the binary's rows kill over 99% of cells biochemically, so the arm that kills everything has nothing left to earn immune kills on. That comparison ranks survivors, not immunogenicity.

## The registered prediction this lands on

`PREREGISTRATION.md`'s **P5** registers a falsification threshold on exactly this quantity: *"DAMP release and DC maturation per dead cell agree within 1.5x between dense (SDT or 3D) and sparse (RSL3) kill"*. Both readouts, measured:

| phenotype | DAMP per dead cell | DC maturation per dead cell |
|---|--:|--:|
| OXPHOS | 1.0748x | 1.0523x |
| Persister | 1.1332x | 1.0950x |

**Both readouts agree within 1.5x in every readable phenotype, the largest being 1.1332x -- so the model's own per-cell output satisfies P5's registered falsification condition.** Read that precisely: P5 is a prediction about BIOLOGY, to be tested by experiment E3, and nothing here falsifies the biology. What it establishes is that the model does not predict what P5 says it predicts. P5's "Quantitative model output" line states a per-cell ratio of "about 4:1 in 3D (down from the 2D 104:1 ceiling)" and the manuscript already records those as total immune-kill ratios rather than per-cell readouts; measured, the per-cell output is the null P5 would be falsified by.

**The threshold is not amended and must not be.** It is parsed from the registration here rather than restated, so this page tracks the registered band instead of carrying its own. Amending a registered threshold after measuring against it is the thing preregistration exists to prevent; the manuscript declined to amend it for the same reason, and so does this.

## Scope

- **32 of the 35 comparisons are panel-sweep CONDITIONS, not independent replicates.** They vary phenotype, depth, hypoxia, stroma and pH, but they all run the same single-cell biochemistry through the same `calculate_damp_release`, as does `sim-icd`. The committed 2D spatial archive is the one row on a different code path. So the count measures how little the ratio moves across the conditions the engine can pose, not how many separate times the claim was tested.
- `damp_per_lp` is 1.0 and uncalibrated, so every ratio here is a property of the model and not a measurement of immunogenicity.
- Nothing is recalibrated and no parameter is changed. What changed is what the engine's comments and the binary's README say about the measurement.
- The manuscript already declines the per-cell reading -- Section 8.2 calls the linear LP-to-DAMP assumption "not a measurement of per-cell immunogenicity" and Figure 16's caption says the 104:1 "compares total immune-kill counts, not DAMP potency or immunogenicity per dead cell" -- so no manuscript number rests on the refuted band, and the manuscript is not edited.
- The totals DO differ by roughly three orders of magnitude between these two arms, and that is the kill count, which is the quantity the manuscript reports.

Verdict: `direction_holds_magnitude_refuted`.
