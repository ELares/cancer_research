# Diagnostic-to-therapy chains at census scale

Generated by `scripts/census_diagnostic_chains.py`. 18,490 of 4,403,994 selected census records (0.42%) match at least one of the 10 chains.

## What changed, and what the comparison is worth

The corpus column reports 240 of 4,830 (5.0%). The two percentages are NOT comparable as rates: the corpus was retrieved by mechanism keyword and the census arm reads the selected MeSH-indexed records, so the corpus is enriched for the mechanisms its queries named and depleted for everything else. What IS comparable is the per-chain ordering and, in particular, which chains return nothing.

The instrument is nearly the same. The published figure was matched over title + MeSH + PubTator annotations + abstract, and the census carries every channel but the annotations. Removing that channel from the corpus arm does not change the count of records matching at least one chain (240). The corpus arm reproduces the published aggregate count of 240; agreement on that count alone does not establish an identical matcher or article cohort. 75.2% of census records carry an abstract at all.

None of the per-chain counts changes in this corpus. Equal counts do not make the input channels identical.

These annotation comparisons describe the retrieved corpus; they do not estimate an annotation effect in the census.

## The ordering, not the rate

Ranks are descriptive of the selected records; equal counts receive their average tied rank.

Ranking the same 10 chains by each column disagrees on 4 of 10 by three places or more:

- `her2-testing-to-trastuzumab`: corpus rank 4, census rank 1
- `egfr-mutation-to-egfr-inhibitor`: corpus rank 6, census rank 2
- `brca-mutation-to-parp-inhibitor`: corpus rank 2, census rank 5
- `neoantigen-profiling-to-mrna-vaccine`: corpus rank 1, census rank 7

These differences do not by themselves identify their cause or establish an ordering beyond the selected populations.

## The zeros

2 chain(s) return zero on the corpus and none returns zero on the census: kras-g12c-mutation-to-sotorasib, sstr-imaging-to-prrt. That is the disclaimer the manuscript attached to this section, now measured: those zeros were a property of a retrieval built around mechanism keywords, not a statement about the literature. Every chain is expressed in the indexed cancer literature.

## What this still cannot say

Chain membership is keyword co-occurrence in title, MeSH and abstract. It says a paper mentions a therapy and either a diagnostic or a targetable feature; it does not say the paper USES one to select the other. The layer measures where the literature pairs the two vocabularies, not where biomarker-directed treatment is established practice.
