# Diagnostic-to-therapy chains at census scale

Generated by `scripts/census_diagnostic_chains.py`. 18,490 of 4,403,994 census records (0.42%) match at least one of the 10 chains.

## What changed, and what the comparison is worth

The corpus column reports 240 of 4,830 (5.0%). The two percentages are NOT comparable as rates: the corpus was retrieved by mechanism keyword and the census is the whole MeSH-indexed cancer literature, so the corpus is enriched for the mechanisms its queries named and depleted for everything else. What IS comparable is the per-chain ordering and, in particular, which chains return nothing.

The instrument is nearly the same. The published figure was matched over title + MeSH + PubTator annotations + abstract, and the census carries every channel but the annotations. Removing that channel from the corpus arm costs nothing at all (0 records), so the two columns are built from the same channels for every chain here. The corpus arm also reproduces the published 240 exactly, so the matcher is the one the manuscript reported. 75.2% of census records carry an abstract at all.

## The ordering, not the rate

Ranking the same ten chains by each column disagrees on 4 of 10 by three places or more:

- `her2-testing-to-trastuzumab`: corpus rank 4, census rank 1
- `egfr-mutation-to-egfr-inhibitor`: corpus rank 6, census rank 2
- `brca-mutation-to-parp-inhibitor`: corpus rank 2, census rank 5
- `neoantigen-profiling-to-mrna-vaccine`: corpus rank 1, census rank 7

The manuscript read its own ordering as a map of where the corpus had translational depth, and said so. The census says which of those readings were about the field: the chains that rise are the ones whose literature the mechanism queries never went looking for.

## The zeros

2 chain(s) return zero on the corpus and none returns zero on the census: kras-g12c-mutation-to-sotorasib, sstr-imaging-to-prrt. That is the disclaimer the manuscript attached to this section, now measured: those zeros were a property of a retrieval built around mechanism keywords, not a statement about the literature. Every chain is expressed in the indexed cancer literature.

## What this still cannot say

Chain membership is keyword co-occurrence in title, MeSH and abstract. It says a paper mentions a therapy and either a diagnostic or a targetable feature; it does not say the paper USES one to select the other. The layer measures where the literature pairs the two vocabularies, not where biomarker-directed treatment is established practice.
