# Is "MeSH has no descriptor" a statement about MeSH?

Generated by `scripts/descriptor_absence_audit.py` over the census's own descriptor vocabulary: **28,754 distinct MeSH descriptors** across 4,403,994 indexed cancer records. 9 mechanisms are reported throughout this project as *not measurable at census scale*, and 16 sites across the generated pages and the manuscript give the reason as MeSH having no descriptor for them. Measured below, 10 of those over-claimed and are corrected here; 6 were about mechanisms whose verdict really is `absent` and are left exactly as written.

**That is not what any of those sites measures.** Every consumer derives it from `analysis/mesh-mechanism-map.yaml` listing no usable descriptor -- a handwritten file whose own header records that its counts were verified against a corpus of 4,830 records. What is measured is *this file names none*; what is carried is *MeSH has none*, about a population 911 times larger.

So the check here is as wide as the claim. **Arm A** is the production rule: a descriptor is a candidate when the mechanism's own `MECHANISM_KEYWORDS` accept its NAME under `tag_articles`' `text_matches_keyword`, the same live matcher `mechanism_recall.py` uses to decide leakiness. **Arm B** is a looser token-subset control with plural stripping and 15 generic words dropped from the keyword side, so an absence is an absence under both and arm A's own blind spot -- it is a substring rule, so a plural keyword cannot reach a singular descriptor -- is measured rather than assumed away. Neither arm is semantic: both are rules over descriptor NAMES, so a descriptor meaning the mechanism in different words is invisible to them exactly as it is to a hand list. Wider is not complete.

## The verdict

**3 of 9 are refuted**: the census carries a name-exact MeSH descriptor for `disulfidptosis` (swept into 9 corrected sentences), `electrolysis` (swept into 7 corrected sentences), `targeted-protein-degradation` (swept into 8 corrected sentences). Each is volume-measurable at census scale, and each was named in a sentence saying MeSH has no descriptor for it.

The remaining 3 + 3 split into two verdicts that were being reported as one word, and `CLAUDE.md` already records that they must stay apart. **Absent** -- nothing names the mechanism and nothing contains it: `bioelectric`, `frequency-therapy`, `ttfields`. **Over-broad** -- a descriptor contains the mechanism but means far more, so a count of it over-counts, which was never absence: `cold-atmospheric-plasma`, `cuproptosis`, `radioligand-therapy`.

| mechanism | verdict | arm A | arm B | volume-measurable | pool | recall-measurable |
|---|---|--:|--:|---|--:|---|
| `bioelectric` | absent | 0 | 6 | no | -- | no |
| `cold-atmospheric-plasma` | over-broad | 0 | 4 | no | -- | no |
| `cuproptosis` | over-broad | 0 | 0 | no | -- | no |
| `disulfidptosis` | name-exact-leaky | 1 | 1 | yes | 99 | no |
| `electrolysis` | name-exact-leaky | 1 | 3 | yes | 85 | no |
| `frequency-therapy` | absent | 0 | 0 | no | -- | no |

| `ttfields` | absent | 0 | 0 | no | -- | no |

**A name-exact descriptor is not automatically a clean count**, and one of the refuted rows shows it: `electrolysis` carries a descriptor whose own pool reaches back past 1990, when the modality did not exist, so its volume is an upper bound. That is the same vintage trap `census-unnamed-modalities.md` measures on the keyword arm, and finding a descriptor does not escape it.

**Volume-measurable and recall-measurable are different claims, and collapsing them is half of why this went unnoticed.** A descriptor whose name trips the mechanism's keywords is LEAKY by `mechanism_recall.py`'s own classifier, so it cannot serve as an independent reference label and that page's headline is right to exclude it. It can carry a census volume count perfectly well, which is what `census_mechanism_profile.py` reports for every measurable mechanism and what the sentences below deny is possible. The recall verdict survives for all 9; the absence claim does not.

## The handwritten verdict, checked where it is checkable

Whether a broad descriptor CONTAINS a mechanism or merely RESEMBLES it is a judgement about MeSH semantics that no rule over names can make, so the verdict stays in the handwritten map. What this page checks is the half that is checkable: every descriptor the map names must exist in the census vocabulary -- `atlas_thesis_rank.py` found several of its own stems matching nothing -- `absent` is refused where a covering descriptor is named or a name-exact one is found, `over-broad` is refused where a name-exact one is found, and `name-exact-leaky` is refused unless arm A finds one and the map lists it.

All checks pass: **True**.

**2 of those thresholds are INERT on the current data and are declared rather than left to look load-bearing**: `POOL_FLOOR` at 30 (every leaf found clears it), `OVER_BROAD_MULTIPLE x POOL_FLOOR` at 90 (every covering descriptor clears it). An inert floor reachable only through a fixture is a recorded defect class here, so each is driven by a doctored vocabulary in `tests/test_descriptor_absence_audit.py` and its committed VALUE is bounded -- because a sweep that deletes a filter nothing reaches changes no output at all.

## What this does not establish

**A descriptor existing is not a mechanism being measurable** in the sense the corrected sentences need. The deliverable is that the absence assertion is false, not that these mechanisms are now countable alongside the rest: every leaf found is leaky, so none can carry a concordance check, and a recent descriptor's census volume is dominated by indexing vintage -- the lesson `analysis/atlas-recent-window.md` records, where a third of significantly-rising descriptors are flat against a recent comparator.

**Both arms are name rules, so neither bounds MeSH's semantic coverage.** A descriptor meaning a mechanism under words the project does not use is invisible to arm A, arm B and the hand list alike. What changed is the scope of the check, from the candidates an author recalled to every descriptor in the census whose name the mechanism's own production vocabulary accepts.

**Nothing here touches the simulation.** No parameter is constrained and `analysis/identifiability-report.json`'s data-conditioned-headline count stays where it is.

**And the vocabulary is a snapshot.** It is the descriptor set of one PubMed baseline, so an absence verdict here has the same expiry the claims it corrects had: `Disulfidptosis` carried no census records before 2024. The difference is that this page's verdicts are re-derived from a committed vocabulary on every render, so refreshing the census is what it takes to re-check them.
