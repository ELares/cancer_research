# Measuring a mechanism keyword before adding it

Over **7,727,217 indexed titles and abstracts**, top 200 matches inspected per candidate.

## Does the measure discriminate?

**No.** The worst real candidate (27%) does not beat the best control (43%), so this measure is not separating a specific phrase from a generic one and **no verdict in the table below should be acted on.**

The binding control is `helicobacter pylori` at 43% over 18,961 records. It sits above 2 of the 10 real candidates (`bacterial outer membrane vesicle`, `magnetotactic bacteria`), so 8 DO clear it.

The decision rule is the strict one and was fixed in the docstring before the result: the WORST real candidate must beat the BEST control, because a measure that ranks a cause above some cures is not one to admit a therapy keyword on. Reporting only the 8 that clear it would be choosing the flattering half of the same table.

Two distinct reasons, and the second is the one that cannot be tuned away. First, this corpus is MeSH tree C04 plus an adjacent extension, so it holds cancer papers only: an organism word cannot drag in the infection literature, because that literature was never admitted -- which is why `salmonella typhimurium` and `probiotic` are not controls here at all. Second, and decisively, a microbe that CAUSES cancer produces titles naming both a microbe and a cancer just as reliably as one that treats it. *Helicobacter pylori* and gastric carcinoma is the clearest case in medicine. Title co-occurrence cannot separate cause from cure, and this mechanism is a therapy class, so the measure is answering a different question from the one asked.

The consequence is acted on rather than noted: **no keyword was added to the taxonomy on this evidence.** The sample below exists because the remaining route is reading.

## What this does not establish

Title aboutness is a PROXY for topical precision, not a measurement of it. A paper can be about a mechanism its title does not name, and a title can name a concept the paper only surveys. Precision is decided by reading, so a deterministic sample of 10 records per candidate is written to `mechanism-keyword-sample.tsv` with an empty verdict column; nothing here fills it in.
